Whisper Dictation VP

AI-powered voice dictation for macOS. Speak instead of typing — the text appears wherever your cursor is, in any app.

💰 Free, forever: it runs on Groq's free API. Create your key at console.groq.com in 1 minute and dictate without paying anything — no subscriptions. The app will ask for it on first launch.

🌐 Speak your language — automatic detection (one language per dictation) or pin one of 16:

Spanish · English · Ukrainian · French · German · Italian · Portuguese · Chinese · Russian · Arabic · Hindi · Japanese · Korean · Turkish · Polish · Dutch

  • Double-tap the dictation key to record; single tap to stop and paste
  • AI Format: removes filler words and fixes punctuation automatically
  • Personal dictionary for names and terms AI tends to get wrong
  • Interactive history, configurable hotkey, new-version notices
  • 100% native app — no Python, no Terminal windows
