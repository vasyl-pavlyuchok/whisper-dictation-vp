Whisper Dictation VP

Voice dictation for macOS powered by AI.

New in v3: 100% native app — no Python required, no Terminal windows.

  • Double-tap right Option to start recording
  • Speak freely — no need to hold any key
  • Single tap to stop and paste automatically
  • Automatic language detection (Spanish, English, Ukrainian and 14 more)
  • AI Format: removes filler words and fixes punctuation (free with Groq)
  • Personal dictionary, interactive history, configurable hotkey
  • Starts automatically at login

On first launch you will be asked for your API key (Groq is free and recommended).
